HOW TO READ THIS
----------------------------------------
  Same logic as Experiment 7. If restricting to well-documented
  poets here also SHRINKS the mixed-cluster percentage (down from
  26%), that's a second, independent piece of evidence for the
  sample-size explanation. If it stays flat or grows, that's a
  second piece of evidence against it -- consistent with what the
  minor-poets-only test (which INCREASED to 39%) already suggested.
