  to real surahs, that confirms the earlier finding was about
  clustering mechanics, not genuine stylistic overlap.
  Exp 18-30 apply the same 'lone point' reasoning to AllPoets,
  35Vector, and 19Vector -- each is a single aggregate point facing
  many Quranic units, so cluster membership alone should be read
  alongside the direct similarity numbers (Exp 18, 23, 24), not
  instead of them.
