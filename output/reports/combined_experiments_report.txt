HOW TO READ THIS
--------------------------------------------------
  Poetry and Quranic text are different genres, eras, and
  registers on their face. If the Quran/surahs land in their
  own separate cluster(s), that is the EXPECTED result -- it
  mainly confirms genre difference, not anything about
  authorship. The more informative signal is the opposite:
  any poet landing in the same cluster as the Quran, or
  surahs scattering broadly across poet clusters instead of
  staying together, would be the surprising/notable finding.
  This method measures writing-style similarity; it cannot
  establish or rule out claims about authorship.
