WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SHOW
----------------------------------------
  - This measures WRITING-STYLE similarity/difference between
    surahs, and checks if that lines up with the already-known
    Meccan/Medinan classification (a low ARI vs. Meccan/Medinan
    just means clusters follow some OTHER pattern, not that
    they are meaningless).
  - It does NOT determine authorship. Single authors commonly
    show large stylistic shifts across years/contexts; shared
    style across decades is also common. Clustering alone
    cannot distinguish between these explanations.
  - The randomization test only tells you whether the observed
    cluster structure exceeds what noise alone would produce --
    it does not speak to WHY that structure exists.
